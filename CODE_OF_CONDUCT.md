# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in ZIPMarkdown harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, respect for differing viewpoints, responsibility for mistakes, and focus on what is best for the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized attention, insults, intimidation, doxxing, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers must clarify and enforce these standards fairly and privately. Reports must use the private channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); the maintainer must fill that channel before public release. Maintainers may remove or reject comments, commits, issues, and contributions that violate this code and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
